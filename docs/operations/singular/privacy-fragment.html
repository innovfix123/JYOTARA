Optional Singular marketing measurement

In Singular-enabled builds, Marketing measurement is a separate, optional Account setting and is off by default. If you enable it, Singular receives installation and session identifiers, device/app and network information, successful sign-in and completed-chat events, and the amounts and currency of verified recharges to measure marketing performance. We restrict advertising identifiers and third-party data sharing. We do not send names, phone numbers, email addresses, birth details or chat content to Singular.

Turning this setting off stops new Singular tracking. It does not delete information previously received by Singular; contact us for privacy or deletion requests. Client marketing reports may omit delayed payments and are not a replacement for our payment ledger. See Singular privacy information.
