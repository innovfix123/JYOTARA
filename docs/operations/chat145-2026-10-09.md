## Tracking and privacy

Migration `0027_service_cost_tracking.sql` separates account-bound operational request/provider records from expiring answer recovery. Actual dispatches, repairs, retries, cache outcomes, safe error codes, provider latency, token usage, reported USD/credits and wallet receipt outcomes can be traced. Unknown provider expenses remain null; credits are not assumed to be rupees. The read-only `service-cost-summary.mjs` reports known-cost counts alongside sums.

Activity events link the message batch, receipt, answer and presentation through an opaque request reference. They contain action/outcome metadata rather than birth fields, OTPs, credentials or message text. Singular and marketing events do not receive chat contents.

Future questions and replies are stored separately only under explicit `research-conversation-v2` consent, encrypted and retained for at most 90 days. Legacy v1 remains question-only. Account/profile deletion removes linked records; deletion races cannot recreate them. Account-bound operational metadata expires after 90 days; anonymous daily cost totals after 365 days. This does not backfill expired historical costs or establish billable minutes or business profit.

## Model comparison

Eight bounded synthetic editor-only attempts compared GPT 6.1 Sol and the current Gemini 2.5 Flash on English, Tamil, Tanglish and a correction/boundary case. GPT passed all four sampled validators and manual review. Gemini English was faster, Tamil timed out after 35 seconds, correction omitted required source quotes, and accepted Tanglish strengthened conditional wording in manual review. This sample does not prove general model superiority or production accuracy.

GPT sample latency was approximately 6–15 seconds. Four-call mean reported inference cost was $0.0053704, approximately ₹0.48 at an illustrative ₹90/USD; three inputs were cached. Known total benchmark cost was $0.0244798 plus one unknown Gemini timeout. These figures exclude Divine, funding/FX fees, payment charges, hosting, marketing and other overhead. No guaranteed reply-time or per-minute-profit claim follows from them.
